# **App Name**: Fridge Scan

## Core Features:

- Image Capture & Upload: Allow users to take a photo of their fridge contents or upload an existing image from their device.
- AI Item Identification: Utilize the Gemini API to analyze the uploaded image and identify discernible food items within the fridge.
- Identified Item List Display: Present the user with a list of food items identified by the AI tool, clearly displayed for review.
- Item Editing/Confirmation: Enable users to easily edit, add, or remove items from the AI-generated list to ensure accuracy.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A fresh, balanced green (#60AC39) to evoke a sense of health and freshness for a food inventory application. This shade offers good readability against a lighter background.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated green (#F2F4F0) providing a clean, bright, and airy feel that mimics the interior of a well-organized fridge, ensuring visual comfort.
- Accent color: A vibrant lime green (#63EB47) with higher saturation and brightness than the primary color, designed to highlight important actions or call-to-action buttons, drawing the user's attention effectively.
- Font: 'Inter', a grotesque sans-serif, chosen for its modern, clean, and objective appearance, ensuring excellent readability for item lists and functional elements.
- Use clear, simple, line-art icons that are intuitive and directly represent their function (e.g., camera icon for capture, list icon for display).
- Employ a clean, card-based layout for displaying identified food items, ensuring each item is distinct and easy to manage, with a prominent area for image upload/capture.
- Implement subtle loading animations or progress indicators when an image is being processed by the AI, providing immediate feedback to the user.
